Our Story

Built With Love

The Mirewood event venue is special because it was built with the love of our owners, Eric and Traci Pulley. In fact, Eric and Traci were the very first to renew their vows at the property and ensure it was filled with love for all others who use it. Now, The Mirewood is an event venue based in Robertson County, Tennessee, open to all types of beautiful celebrations, large and small.

When the Pulley's bought 3 acres in Robertson County early in their marriage, they knew they wanted to celebrate love on this land. After purchasing additional land behind that original plot, they have since built the Mirewood. A painstaking labor of love, the Mirewood is now available for celebrations of all kinds: weddings, birthdays, baby showers, corporate events, and more!
